Privacy Policy

Inbox Aggregator · Last updated September 28, 2026

Inbox Aggregator is a personal app built by Aaron Cheung that brings messages from several email accounts into one inbox, lets the account owner search them with an AI assistant, and lets them write and send email from their connected accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

  • Your email address, to identify each connected account.
  • Your Gmail messages: sender, recipients, subject, date, message text, labels (such as read/unread), and attachment names, types and sizes.
  • Other email accounts you connect with a password (IMAP): the same details for messages in the inbox and sent folders, plus the server name and password needed to sign in.

The app sends email only when you write it and press Send, from the account you choose. It does not modify email in any connected account, and deletes email only in one case: when a temporary address you made in the app expires or you delete it, the emails that address received are deleted.

How the data is used

  • To show your messages from all connected accounts in one inbox.
  • To let you search your messages and ask questions about them.
  • To send the emails you write, including replies and forwards, with any files you attach. An email waits briefly after you press Send so it can be undone; its content is removed from the app's database, and its attached files from the app's file storage, once it has been sent or undone. A forward's original attachments are read from your mailbox when it is sent, and not stored.
  • To make temporary email addresses on your own domain when you ask for one. Each forwards mail to your mailbox until it expires; then the address is removed and the emails it received are deleted.

Your data is used only to provide these features to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Where the data is stored

  • Messages are stored in a private database hosted by Supabase. Files attached to an email being sent wait in private Supabase file storage until it is sent.
  • Google sign-in tokens and email account passwords are encrypted before they are stored.

Services that process your data

To power search and the AI assistant, parts of your messages are sent to these services:

  • OpenAI, to create search indexes (embeddings) from message text.
  • Anthropic, to answer your questions using the most relevant messages, and to help write an email when you ask it to.

These services receive only what is needed for each request. Under their API terms, they do not use that data to train their models.

Google API Services

Inbox Aggregator's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page. For accounts connected with a password, changing that password cuts off access. To have your stored messages deleted, email contact@aaroncheung.me, and all data for your account will be removed.
